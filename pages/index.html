Mathematical Formulations

Formal definitions of optimization objectives, loss functions, and statistical tests.

Sub-linear TF-IDF Vectorization

Term frequency with logarithmic dampening and smoothed inverse document frequency:

$$\mathrm{tf}_{\mathrm{sub}}(t,d) = 1 + \log\bigl(\#(t \in d)\bigr)$$
$$\mathrm{idf}(t) = \log\!\left(\frac{1+N}{1+\mathrm{df}(t)}\right) + 1$$

Regularized Logistic Objectives

Ridge (L2), Lasso (L1), and ElasticNet penalties:

$$\mathcal{L} = -\sum_{i=1}^{n}\bigl[y_i\log\hat{p}_i + (1-y_i)\log(1-\hat{p}_i)\bigr] + \lambda\bigl[\alpha\|\mathbf{w}\|_1 + \tfrac{1-\alpha}{2}\|\mathbf{w}\|_2^2\bigr]$$

L1 (\(\alpha=1\)): 83.67% sparsity (57/349 features). L2 (\(\alpha=0\)): champion. ElasticNet (\(\alpha=0.5\)): 65.33% sparsity.

Tree Impurity & Cost-Complexity Pruning

$$G(t) = 1 - \sum_{k=1}^{K} p_k^2, \quad H(t) = -\sum_{k=1}^{K} p_k \log_2 p_k$$
$$R_\alpha(T) = R(T) + \alpha\,|\widetilde{T}|$$

Minimal cost-complexity pruning with \(\mathrm{ccp\_alpha}\) prevents overfitting on small training sets.

Calibration & Statistical Drift Tests

Platt sigmoid calibration and Brier proper scoring rule:

$$\tilde{p} = \sigma(a\cdot p + b), \quad \mathrm{BS} = \frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n}(p_i - y_i)^2$$

Kolmogorov–Smirnov and Population Stability Index:

$$D_{n,m} = \sup_x |F_n(x) - G_m(x)|, \quad \mathrm{PSI} = \sum_b (q_b - p_b)\ln\!\frac{q_b}{p_b}$$

L1 Sparsity Demonstration

Lasso regularization drove 292 out of 349 TF-IDF features to exactly zero, retaining only 57 discriminative vocabulary terms. This achieves extreme compression and full interpretability — every prediction can be explained by a short list of weighted words.
