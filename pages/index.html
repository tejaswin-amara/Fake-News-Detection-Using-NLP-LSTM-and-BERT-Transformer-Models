Unsupervised Learning & Corpus Geometry (CO4)

Exploration of latent document representations through geometric projection, clustering, and anomaly isolation.

K-Means Clustering

Evaluated for k in [2, 6]. Optimal silhouette score reached at k=5 (0.0124) with inertia 47.18. Confirms natural topic groupings across geopolitical, economic, and sensationalist domains.

Hierarchical Agglomeration

Ward linkage achieves 0.0155 silhouette score with natural division into 19 and 37 document sub-trees. Complete and average linkage models confirm distinct cluster separation.

Isolation Forest Anomalies

Identified anomalous articles with contamination rate of 0.05. Anomalies predominantly correlate with heavily capitalized, punctuation-dense, and unverified viral chain text.
